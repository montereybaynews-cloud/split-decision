Confidence percentages and 50/50 splits are your own estimates, not guarantees — this tool only organizes the combinatorics (round robins, stake splits, blended win probability via simulation) based on the numbers you enter. Parlays and round robins compound risk: every added leg lowers the chance the whole ticket cashes, even when each individual leg is likely. Bet only what you can afford to lose. This runs entirely in your browser; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
